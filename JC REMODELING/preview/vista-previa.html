What we install

Flooring Installation Services in Miami

These are the flooring types we install, with notes on how each one performs in Miami's humid climate.

Porcelain & Ceramic Tile

Hard-wearing, easy to clean and cool underfoot. Porcelain absorbs very little water, so it holds up well to Miami humidity, spills and heavy foot traffic. Wood-look planks, large-format tiles and mosaics are all popular choices.

  • Large-format and wood-look tile
  • Water-resistant, durable surface
  • Stays cool in hot weather

Best for: Kitchens, bathrooms, living areas and entryways

Luxury Vinyl Plank (LVP)

Realistic wood-look planks that are water-resistant or waterproof, depending on the product, comfortable underfoot and quick to install. A smart option for busy households and moisture-prone rooms.

  • Water-resistant to waterproof options
  • Wood and stone looks
  • Comfortable, quieter underfoot

Best for: Kitchens, bedrooms, living areas and whole-home updates

Laminate Flooring

An affordable way to get the look of hardwood, with a scratch-resistant surface and click-lock installation. Best for dry interior rooms; in Miami, choose a water-resistant laminate.

  • Budget-friendly wood look
  • Scratch-resistant surface
  • Fast click-lock installation

Best for: Bedrooms, offices and dry living areas

Wood & Engineered Wood

Warm, timeless and long-lasting. Because Miami's humidity can make solid wood move, engineered wood is often the better fit, and careful subfloor prep and moisture testing matter most.

  • Classic, natural look
  • Engineered options for humidity
  • Adds lasting value to your home

Best for: Living rooms, dining rooms and bedrooms

Baseboards & Trim

The finishing detail that completes the room. New baseboards, shoe molding and transitions give your floors a clean, polished edge.

  • Baseboard replacement
  • Shoe molding and quarter round
  • Clean transitions between rooms

Best for: Any room getting new floors

Miami flooring guide

Best Flooring for Miami Homes and Condos

South Florida's heat, humidity and concrete-slab construction affect which floors last. Use this quick comparison to narrow down your options.

Flooring types compared for Miami homes
Flooring type Humidity and moisture Best for Keep in mind
Porcelain & ceramic tile Excellent. Very low water absorption. Kitchens, bathrooms, living areas Grout needs periodic cleaning; harder and cooler underfoot.
Luxury vinyl plank (LVP) Very good. Many products are waterproof. Whole-home use, busy households Quality varies by product; needs a flat subfloor.
Laminate Fair. Choose a water-resistant line. Bedrooms and dry living areas Avoid wet areas; edges can swell if flooded.
Wood & engineered wood Moderate. Engineered is more stable than solid. Living rooms, dining rooms, bedrooms Subfloor moisture testing is essential.

Good to know

Flooring Installation FAQs

Last updated: October 2026

What is the best flooring for Miami's humid climate?

Porcelain tile and luxury vinyl plank are the most dependable choices for Miami's heat and humidity, because both resist moisture far better than solid wood or standard laminate. Engineered wood can also work when the subfloor is tested and prepared correctly. During your free estimate we recommend the best material for each room.

How much does flooring installation cost in Miami?

The cost depends on the material you choose, the size of the area, the condition of the subfloor and whether old flooring or baseboards need to be removed. The material is usually the biggest factor. The quickest way to get an accurate number is a free estimate, where we visit your space and quote your specific project.

How long does a flooring installation take?

Small rooms with click-lock vinyl plank or laminate can often be finished in about a day, while tile and wood usually take longer because of prep, setting and curing time. Whole-home projects take longer still. Ask us for a realistic schedule when you request your estimate.

Can new flooring be installed over my existing floor?

Sometimes. Luxury vinyl plank and laminate can often go over a flat, sound existing floor, but tile, wood and any uneven or damaged surface usually need to be removed or leveled first. We check the existing surface during your estimate and explain what your project needs.

What flooring works best in a Miami condo?

Many Miami condo associations require approval and a sound-rated underlayment before flooring work begins, so check your building's rules first. Porcelain tile and luxury vinyl plank are both popular condo choices. Tell us your building and floor level when you request your estimate.

Is luxury vinyl plank better than laminate?

For most Miami homes, yes. LVP handles moisture better than standard laminate, which can swell when it gets wet. Laminate is still a budget-friendly option for dry rooms, and water-resistant laminate lines are available.

Do you install baseboards and trim?

Yes. Baseboards and trim are part of our flooring service, so your new floors get a clean, finished edge. Mention it in your estimate request and we will include it in the plan.
